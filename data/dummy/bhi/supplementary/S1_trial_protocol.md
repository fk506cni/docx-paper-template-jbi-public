## 1. Interaction conditions

| Condition | Definition |
|:------------|:-----------|
| Staring | The subject looks directly at the apparatus for the whole trial. |
| Gentle touch | The subject rests one hand on the apparatus body, avoiding the sharp corners. |
| Looking away | The subject faces away from the apparatus and thinks about something else. |

## 2. Scoring of the exasperation term

The supervising professor is asked to sigh naturally, and a listener scores each sigh on an
ordinal scale from one to five. Where two listeners disagreed by more than one point, the
trial was rescored after a short break. Trials in which the professor left the room were
scored at the maximum and were not excluded.
