2026-07-30

To the Editor-in-Chief\
BioHealth Informatics

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled **"Quantifying the Tremendous Hunch with a
Purpose-Built Apparatus and a New Dimensional Scale"** for consideration as an Original Article
in BioHealth Informatics.

<!-- (1) なぜ本稿が BHI に掲載されるべきか -->

The tremendous hunch is reported often enough to need no defence as a phenomenon, yet no
instrument records it and no scale expresses it. We describe an apparatus that logs three
component quantities, a dimensional scale built from them, and a 20-trial evaluation in which
the ordering of three interaction conditions held in every trial while the absolute scores
moved by an order of magnitude. The methodological point — that a composite index mixing
instrumented and human-scored quantities can be trustworthy for ranking long before it is
trustworthy for measurement — is one your readership meets whenever a clinical score is built
this way, which is why we believe the work belongs in BioHealth Informatics rather than in a
general instrumentation venue.

<!-- (2) 雑誌ポリシーに関わる論点 -->

Two points bear on journal policy. The study involves neither human samples nor patient data,
so no ethics review was applicable; the sole subject is an author, and the manuscript says so.
The trial logs and apparatus drawings are fictional, and the data availability statement in the
manuscript says that plainly rather than claiming a restriction that does not exist.

<!-- (3) 潜在的な競合利益の申告 -->

The authors declare that they have no competing interests related to this work. The supervising
professor, whose degree of exasperation appears in the denominator of the proposed scale, is
not an author and had no role in the analysis.

<!-- (4) 未発表かつ他誌へ同時投稿していないことの確認 -->

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration
for publication elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the submitted version and agree
to its submission to BioHealth Informatics.

We look forward to hearing from you.
