# Response to Reviewers

We thank the reviewers for their constructive comments. Our point-by-point responses follow.
Quoted reviewer comments are in italics; manuscript changes are noted with line references.

## Reviewer 1

> *Comment 1.1 — The abstract does not state the number of trials.*

We have revised the Abstract to state that **twenty** trials were recorded across three
interaction conditions. (Abstract, "Results".)

> *Comment 1.2 — Please clarify why exasperation is squared.*

We added a sentence to Methods explaining that squaring the term in the denominator makes the
effect of an unmoved supervisor visible in the formula rather than in the discussion.
(Methods, "Combination rule".)

## Reviewer 2

> *Comment 2.1 — Are the known limitations of the composite score documented?*

Yes. The Discussion now lists the listener-scored term, the unbalanced trial counts, and the
fact that a recomputed score agrees with the reported one by construction.

We believe these revisions address all concerns and improve the manuscript.
