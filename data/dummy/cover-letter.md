2026-07-30

To the Editor-in-Chief,

Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled **"A Measurement Pipeline for the Tremendous
Hunch, from Apparatus to Reported Score"** for consideration as an original article.

The work describes a pipeline that stores every input to a composite score alongside the rule
that combined them, so that a trial can be recomputed later without the apparatus. The
manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal.
All authors have approved the submission and declare no conflicts of interest.

We believe the topic fits the scope of your journal and look forward to your review.

Sincerely,

Taro Yokan, on behalf of all authors
Graduate School of Hunch Engineering, University of the Tremendous
